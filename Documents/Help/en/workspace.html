• Tools and Contents: choose a tool and inspect layers, tables and outputs. A layer context menu offers zoom, table/properties and removal from the view. Removing a display layer does not delete its file.
  • Center tabs: Map, Attribute Table, Tasks, Input Info, About and PDF Atlas Preview. Use the tab bar arrows when space is limited.
  • Tool inspector: Inputs, Climate, Raster and Outputs. Drag splitters to resize panels; window geometry/layout are saved. The application menu can toggle panels or restore the layout.

Drag to pan; scroll or use +/− to zoom. Navigation controls also fit the data and show the world. The pin icon enables point placement: click the map and complete the point dialog, or press Esc to cancel. The coordinate readout is WGS84; tile display uses EPSG:3857, independently of analysis/output CRS.

The small layers icon opens the basemap panel: Esri imagery with/without labels, streets, topographic, light/dark gray, OpenStreetMap or no basemap. The panel identifies the current style and provider. Selecting a style, clicking outside, Close or Esc dismisses it; focus loss alone while taking a screenshot does not. Thumbnails illustrate styles and are not measurements or additional tile downloads.

Attribution remains below the map. New basemap tiles require internet; local points can be displayed without a basemap. A basemap does not select a climate provider. API-key Google/Azure basemaps are absent. Application chrome follows light/dark mode; the geographic map follows the selected basemap style.
