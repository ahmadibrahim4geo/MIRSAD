================================================================================
DATA SOURCE PROFILE: NOAA ISD / GHCN GROUND OBSERVATIONS ARCHIVE
================================================================================
Source Name           : NOAA ISD / GHCN Direct Station Ground Observations Archive
Provider Organization : National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA) / NCEI
Collaborator          : World Meteorological Organization (WMO)
Coverage Start        : 1901 (Standard Synoptic Digital Archive)
Coverage End          : Present (Real-Time Ingestion)
Temporal Resolution   : Sub-hourly, Hourly, 3-hourly, Daily, Monthly
Spatial Resolution    : Point Station Physical Coordinates (WGS84 EPSG:4326)
Total Station Network : 35,000+ cataloged stations (28,095 curated in Atlas Hub)

WHAT THIS SOURCE CAN PROVIDE:
[+] 100% Pure Physical Ground Truth from In-Situ Meteorological Instruments
[+] Ambient Air Temperature at 2m (Mean, Maximum, Minimum)
[+] Dew Point Temperature and Psychrometric Relative Humidity
[+] Dual Barometric Pressure: Station Pressure (PS) and Sea Level Pressure (PSL)
[+] Wind Speed at 10m Mast, Compass Wind Direction, and Peak Wind Gusts
[+] Liquid Precipitation Accumulation (mm), Rain Day Counts, Extreme Storms
[+] Total Cloud Cover (Oktas 0-8) and Ceilometer Cloud Base Ceiling Height
[+] Official Present Weather Phenology (Fog, Dust, Thunderstorm, Rain Types)
[+] Certified Evidentiary Legal Compliance for Audits and Court Forensics

WHAT THIS SOURCE CANNOT PROVIDE:
[-] Surface Solar Radiation Flux (W/m2) for Routine Synoptic Stations
[-] Satellite UV Erythemal Index (Requires Specialized Spectrometers)
[-] Land Surface Skin Temperature (LST - Satellite Sensor Property)
[-] Subsurface Multi-Layer Soil Moisture/Temperature Profiles
[-] 100% Temporal Continuity (Prone to Sensor Outages, Maintenance Gaps, -999)
[-] Gridded Continuous Coverage Across Deserts, Oceans, or Station Voids
