EXECUTIVE SUMMARY / ملخص تنفيذي
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
تعتمد أدوات منظومة مِرصاد (MIRSAD Climate Atlas Generator) على خمسة مصادر رئيسية لاستخراج السلاسل الزمنية
المناخية، وحساب المعدلات المعيارية، وبناء خرائط الراستر الاستيفائية. يوضح هذا الملف الفترات الزمنية 
الفعلية (بداية التغطية ونهايتها) لكل مصدر، والعناصر المناخية التي يوفرها، ونقاط القوة وحالات الاستخدام:

1. Hybrid Engine (النموذج الهجين - الافتراضي):
   - البداية: 1901 (حسب رصد المحطة) | النهاية: الوقت الحاضر (Present)
   - الرصد الأرضي المباشر + استكمال ثغرات الرصد آلياً + دمج عناصر الإشعاع الفضائي.

2. NOAA ISD / GHCN Direct Station Ground Observations:
   - البداية: 1901 (وسجلات بعض المحطات تعود لأواخر القرن 19) | النهاية: الوقت الحاضر (Real-Time)
   - قراءات أجهزة ومستشعرات المحطة الأرضية الحقيقية (الحرارة، الرطوبة، الضغط، الرياح، الأمطار، السحب).

3. NASA POWER API (Agroclimatology / MERRA-2):
   - البداية: 01/01/1981 (البيانات اليومية والشهرية) | 01/01/1984 (الإشعاع الشمسي) | النهاية: الوقت الحاضر
   - النموذج الزراعي العالمي لناسا (دقة شبكية 0.5° × 0.625°)، تغطية مكانية عالمية كاملة للإشعاع والحرارة.

4. Copernicus Climate Data Store (CDS / ERA5 / AgERA5):
   - البداية: 01/01/1940 (ERA5) | 01/01/1950 (ERA5-Land) | 01/01/1979 (AgERA5) | النهاية: الوقت الحاضر
   - مخزن كوبرنيكوس الأوروبي المعتمد دولياً لدى WMO/ECMWF، دقة مكانية 0.1° (~9 كم)، دعم اختياري للرمز الشخصي واستخراج سلس بدون كلمة مرور.

5. Open-Meteo Historical API (ECMWF ERA5 & ERA5-Land Reanalysis):
   - البداية: 01/01/1940 (ERA5) | 01/01/1950 (ERA5-Land عالي الدقة) | النهاية: الوقت الحاضر
   - بوابة سحابية مفتوحة للنموذج الأوروبي ERA5-Land (0.1° ~ 9 إلى 11 كم) ملائمة للمناطق الجبلية والتضاريس.

1. HYBRID ENGINE: NOAA GROUND OBSERVATIONS + NASA / ERA5 GAP-FILLING [DEFAULT]
====================================================================================================
- Source Identifier      : Hybrid: NOAA Station Observations + NASA / ERA5 Gap-Filling
- Operational Role       : Default Engine for NOAA & WMO Ground Weather Stations Tool
- Primary Institutions   : NOAA NCEI, WMO, NASA LaRC, and ECMWF Copernicus C3S
- Temporal Start Year    : 1901 (Bound by station's active commissioning date)
- Temporal End Year      : Present (Updated continuously; 2-5 days latency for gap-filled days)
- Temporal Resolution    : Hourly, Daily, Monthly Climatology, Multi-Year Normals
- Spatial Coverage       : Global (>35,000 WMO/USAF stations worldwide)

A. ARCHITECTURE & FUSION MECHANISM:
   1. Level 1 (Ground Truth Priority):
      The engine extracts certified ground sensor readings directly from the official NOAA ISD
      and GHCN archives.
   2. Level 2 (Seamless Gap-Filling):
      If a physical station experienced sensor maintenance, power failure, or telemetry dropouts
      (NoData / -999 sentinels), the missing timestamps are automatically gap-filled using 
      ECMWF ERA5-Land (1950-Present) or NASA POWER (1981-Present) at the station's exact coordinates.
   3. Level 3 (Radiative Sensor Synthesis):
      Since standard synoptic weather stations rarely feature pyranometers or UV spectrometers,
      Solar Radiation, UV Index, and Land Surface Skin Temperature (LST) are seamlessly retrieved
      from NASA CERES and ERA5-Land.

B. WHAT THIS SOURCE CAN PROVIDE (100% COMPLETE COVERAGE - ALL 19 MODULES):
   [+] Air Temperature (Mean, Max, Min, Diurnal Range)
   [+] Dew Point Temperature & Relative Humidity
   [+] Atmospheric Pressure (Station Pressure & Sea Level Pressure PSL)
   [+] Wind Speed (Mean, Max, Gusts) & Wind Direction (Circular Mean 0-360°)
   [+] Precipitation (Total, Daily Mean, Rain Days, Extremes)
   [+] Cloud Cover (Okta and Area Fraction %)
   [+] Solar Radiation (All-Sky Global Horizontal Irradiance, Clear Sky, Direct Normal, Diffuse)
   [+] UV Index (Erythemal Daily Dose)
   [+] Land Surface Skin Temperature (LST)
   [+] Derived Bioclimatic Submodels: Heat Index (Rothfusz), Wind Chill (NWS),
       De Martonne Aridity, Reference Evapotranspiration (Hargreaves & FAO-56),
       UNEP Aridity, Annual Water Deficit, Walter-Lieth Dry Months, Baseline Anomalies & Trends.

C. WHAT THIS SOURCE CANNOT PROVIDE:
   [-] Pure in-situ ground pyranometer records (radiation synthesized via satellite CERES fusion).
   [-] Multi-layer soil moisture/temperature profiles at physical station towers.
   [-] Raw unadjusted court forensic records (litigation requires unadjusted NOAA ISD direct transcripts).
   [-] Gap-filling protection for historical stations decommissioned before 1940.

D. RECOMMENDED USE CASE:
   The ultimate choice for rigorous scientific and professional GIS atlases. Guarantees true
   ground observations while eliminating all interpolation voids, null raster pixels, or missing
   columns in output geodatabases.

2. NOAA ISD / GHCN DIRECT STATION GROUND OBSERVATIONS ARCHIVE
====================================================================================================
- Source Identifier      : NOAA ISD / GHCN Direct Station Ground Observations Archive
- Primary Institutions   : National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA) / NCEI & WMO
- Temporal Start Year    : 1901 (Global digital synoptic archive; sporadic records from 1850s)
- Temporal End Year      : Present (Near-Real-Time NRT; updated hourly/sub-daily)
- Temporal Resolution    : Sub-hourly, Hourly, 3-Hourly, Daily, Monthly
- Spatial Resolution     : Point Station Physical Coordinates (Lat, Lon, Elevation)
- Global Network Size    : 35,000+ total stations (28,095 active synoptic stations in directory)

A. AVAILABLE MEASURED PARAMETERS:
   [X] Air Temperature (T2M): Dry bulb thermometer readings at ~2 meters height.
   [X] Dew Point Temperature (Td): Wet bulb / hygrometer observations.
   [X] Relative Humidity (RH): Psychrometrically derived from Dry & Wet bulb.
   [X] Atmospheric Pressure: Station Pressure (STP) and Sea Level Pressure (SLP / PSL).
   [X] Wind Speed & Direction: Anemometer speed (m/s, knots) and vane direction (0-360°).
   [X] Precipitation: Tipping bucket and manual rain gauge accumulation (mm).
   [X] Cloud Cover / Sky Condition: Visual and ceilometer cloud coverage (Okta, 0-8).
   [X] Weather Phenomena: Present weather codes (Fog, Haze, Dust Storm, Rain, Thunderstorm).

B. PARAMETERS NOT MEASURED AT STANDARD SYNOPTIC STATIONS:
   [-] Solar Radiation Flux (W/m2): Measured only at specialized BSRN/SURFRAD stations.
   [-] UV Index: Measured only at spectrophotometer stations.
   [-] Land Surface Skin Temperature: Satellite radiometric property (not measured by air probes).

C. RECOMMENDED USE CASE:
   Official meteorological compliance, historical climate validation against national records,
   and research focusing purely on ground thermometer, barometer, and anemometer records.

3. NASA POWER API (AGROCLIMATOLOGY / MERRA-2)
====================================================================================================
- Source Identifier      : NASA POWER API (Agroclimatology / MERRA-2)
- Primary Institution    : NASA Langley Research Center (LaRC) & NASA GMAO
- Primary Models         : GMAO MERRA-2 Assimilation + GEOS-FP-IT + CERES FLASHFlux Satellite
- Temporal Start Date    : 
  * Daily & Monthly Meteorology : 1981-01-01 (January 1, 1981)
  * Surface Solar Radiation     : 1984-01-01 (Continuous global CERES coverage from 2000)
  * Hourly Meteorology          : 2001-01-01 (January 1, 2001)
- Temporal End Date      : Present (Trailing real-time by ~2 to 4 days latency)
- Temporal Resolution    : Hourly, Daily, Monthly, Climatology (30-year Normals)
- Spatial Resolution     : 0.5° Latitude × 0.625° Longitude (~50 km × 60 km grid cells)

A. AVAILABLE PARAMETERS:
   [X] ALLSKY_SFC_SW_DWN : All-Sky Surface Shortwave Solar Radiation (MJ/m2/day or kWh/m2/day)
   [X] CLRSKY_SFC_SW_DWN : Clear-Sky Surface Shortwave Solar Radiation
   [X] ALLSKY_SFC_UVA/B  : UV Erythemal Exposure and UV Index
   [X] T2M, T2M_MAX, MIN : Air Temperature at 2 meters (Mean, Max, Min, Range)
   [X] T2MDEW, RH2M      : Dew Point Temperature and Relative Humidity at 2 meters
   [X] PS                : Surface Atmospheric Pressure (kPa / mbar / hPa)
   [X] WS2M, WS10M, WS50M: Wind Speed at 2m, 10m, and 50m heights
   [X] WD10M             : Wind Direction at 10 meters (0-360°)
   [X] PRECTOTCORR       : Corrected Total Precipitation rate (mm/day)
   [X] CLDTOT            : Total Cloud Area Fraction (%)
   [X] TS                : Earth Skin Surface Temperature (LST)

B. WHAT THIS SOURCE CANNOT PROVIDE:
   [-] Historical data prior to January 1, 1981 (early 20th century 1901-1980 unavailable).
   [-] Instantaneous peak wind gust spikes (provides hourly/daily mean speeds only).
   [-] Mean Sea Level Pressure (PSL) directly (provides surface PS only; requires barometric formula).
   [-] Subsurface soil moisture and soil temperature depth profiles (0-255 cm).
   [-] Fine topographic microclimate resolution in steep mountain ravines (~50 km grid cell).
   [-] Zero-day instant real-time data (trails real-time by 2-4 days).

C. RECOMMENDED USE CASE:
   Renewable energy site prospecting (Solar PV / Wind), agricultural yield and irrigation planning,
   and regional studies across desert or ocean regions where ground stations are sparse.

4. OPEN-METEO HISTORICAL WEATHER API (ECMWF ERA5 & ERA5-LAND REANALYSIS)
====================================================================================================
- Source Identifier      : Open-Meteo Historical API (ERA5 Reanalysis - High-Resolution)
- Primary Institutions   : European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts (ECMWF) / Copernicus C3S
- Primary Models         : ECMWF ERA5 Atmospheric Reanalysis & ERA5-Land Terrestrial Reanalysis
- Temporal Start Date    :
  * ERA5 Global Reanalysis      : 1940-01-01 (January 1, 1940)
  * ERA5-Land Enhanced Surface  : 1950-01-01 (January 1, 1950)
- Temporal End Date      : Present (Trailing real-time by ~2 to 5 days latency)
- Temporal Resolution    : Hourly and Daily
- Spatial Resolution     :
  * ERA5 Global : 0.25° (~28 km)
  * ERA5-Land   : 0.10° (~9 km to 11 km) - Exceptional high spatial resolution

A. AVAILABLE PARAMETERS (WHAT THIS SOURCE CAN PROVIDE):
   [X] Temperature at 2m (Mean, Max, Min) & Apparent Temperature (Heat Index / Wind Chill)
   [X] Dew Point Temperature & Relative Humidity at 2m
   [X] Surface Pressure & Mean Sea Level Pressure (MSLP / PSL)
   [X] Precipitation (Total Precipitation, Rain, Snowfall, Snow Depth)
   [X] Wind Speed at 10m & 100m, Wind Direction, Wind Gusts
   [X] Shortwave Solar Radiation (Global Horizontal) & Direct Radiation
   [X] Total Cloud Cover, Low Cloud Cover, Mid Cloud Cover, High Cloud Cover
   [X] Soil Temperature at 4 depths (0-7 cm, 7-28 cm, 28-100 cm, 100-255 cm)
   [X] Soil Moisture at 4 volumetric depths (m3/m3)
   [X] Reference Evapotranspiration (ET0 FAO-56 Penman-Monteith)

B. WHAT THIS SOURCE CANNOT PROVIDE:
   [-] Historical data prior to January 1, 1940 (early 20th century 1901-1939 unavailable).
   [-] Dedicated satellite UV Erythemal Index (relies on general atmospheric radiative approximation).
   [-] Pure in-situ physical instrument readings (it is a numerical reanalysis model, not ground truth).
   [-] Zero-day instant real-time data (trails real-time by 2-5 days).

C. RECOMMENDED USE CASE:
   High-resolution terrain mapping, mountainous watersheds where 9 km grid captures lapse rates,
   hydrological modeling, soil water balance, and baseline studies spanning 1940 to 1980.

5. COPERNICUS CLIMATE DATA STORE (CDS / ERA5 / AgERA5) [OFFICIAL EUROPEAN REANALYSIS]
====================================================================================================
- Source Identifier      : Copernicus Climate Data Store (CDS / ERA5 / AgERA5)
- Operational Role       : Official ECMWF C3S Reanalysis Provider for Points & Gridded Rasters
- Primary Institutions   : European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts (ECMWF) & Copernicus C3S
- Official Portal        : https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu
- Temporal Start Year    : 1940 (ERA5) | 1950 (ERA5-Land ~9 km) | 1979 (AgERA5)
- Temporal End Year      : Present (Continuous near-real-time with 5-day latency)
- Temporal Resolution    : Hourly, Daily, and Monthly Climatologies
- Spatial Resolution     : 0.1° (~9 km to 11 km) for ERA5-Land & AgERA5; 0.25° (~31 km) for ERA5 Global
- Spatial Coverage       : Global Continuous Gridded Archive (Land, Oceans, Atmosphere)
- Authentication         : Optional Personal Access Token (API Key / Token); Seamless Zero-Auth Fallback

A. WHAT THIS SOURCE CAN PROVIDE:
   [X] International Gold Standard: WMO and IPCC certified reference reanalysis baseline.
   [X] Unbroken 85+ Year Historical Series (1940-Present) with 4D-Var Data Assimilation.
   [X] Air Temperature (Mean, Max, Min, Diurnal Range) & Apparent / Heat Stress Indices.
   [X] Dew Point & Relative Humidity (computed via Magnus thermodynamic equations).
   [X] Sea Level Pressure (PSL) & Surface Atmospheric Pressure (PS).
   [X] Wind Speed at 10m & 100m, Wind Direction, and Maximum Wind Gusts.
   [X] Total Precipitation, Rain, Snowfall, and Snow Depth.
   [X] Multi-Layer Soil Moisture & Temperature (4 layers: 0-7, 7-28, 28-100, 100-255 cm).
   [X] Standard Reference Evapotranspiration (ET0 FAO-56 Penman-Monteith).
   [X] AgERA5 Tailored Agricultural Indicators for crop and vegetation growth models.
   [X] Full Support for Spatial Point Features and Gridded Regional NetCDF Processing.

B. WHAT THIS SOURCE CANNOT PROVIDE:
   [-] Data prior to January 1, 1940 (1901-1939 unavailable in ERA5).
   [-] Not a physical ground sensor (it is a sophisticated numerical reanalysis assimilation model).
   [-] Future climate scenario projections (CMIP6 / IPCC SSPs are excluded in current scope).
   [-] Operational trailing latency of 2 to 5 days.

C. RECOMMENDED USE CASE:
   Authoritative climatological benchmarks, national atlases, agricultural water demand modeling,
   and complex terrain mapping where 9 km resolution captures topography accurately.
