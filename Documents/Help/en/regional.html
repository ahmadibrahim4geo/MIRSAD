1. Select Grid Raster Climate Atlas.
  2. Choose the download AOI polygon/extent and an optional separate clipping study boundary; they serve different purposes.
  3. Request whole months, such as 2024-01-01 through 2024-12-31.
  4. Choose modules, units and output CRS. Choose NASA monthly, CHIRPS or CHELSA. The latter two enumerate original native cell centres inside the download polygon, capped at 20,000 cells. Their monthly rainfall totals are not multiplied by days.
  5. For rasters, select the full pipeline, metric processing CRS, cell size, interpolation and clipping mask; run.

Native source cells are read. Monthly precipitation rates are converted using the actual calendar month's length. Monthly data do not produce synthetic Daily observations. Different variable grids can leave dependencies missing at some coordinates; values are not fabricated to hide this. Regional requests are tiled, with limits of 512 tiles and 20,000 grid points; reduce the AOI if needed.
