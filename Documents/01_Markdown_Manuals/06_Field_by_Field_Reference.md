## Addendum A — October 2026 field additions (UV noon-max, HI seasons, WC opt-in suite, Daily sheet)

132 generated fields `<Prefix>_<Month>_Range` (11 mean-type modules × 12 months:
T, R, PSL, PS, W_Spd, RH, Td, Sol, UV, Cld, ET) measure max−min of each calendar month
across years on the same transformed series as the means (pressure in mbar/hPa, solar in
kWh, UV as WHO noon-max, precipitation as totals, ET via Hargreaves). Registered in
`FIELD_DEFS` (Period Monthly, Statistic Range), routed to the Month sheet and `Month/`
rasters like the monthly means, published only with the independent Monthly Ranges toggle
(default OFF). Wind direction excluded (circular).

### A.1 UV Index: 24h-mean → WHO noon-maximum conversion
NASA POWER `ALLSKY_SFC_UV_INDEX` daily/monthly values are 24-hour means including night zeros.
`compute_point_fields` converts each monthly value via `uv_mean24_to_noonmax`
(`Toolboxes/POWER_Climate_Atlas_Generator_10_8.pyt`, `daylength_hours` + `uv_mean24_to_noonmax`)
before seasonal/annual aggregation; Open-Meteo `uv_index_max` is averaged directly
(`uv_source` flag). New field `UV_Seasonal_Range` (max seasonal noon-max minus min).
Classification Guide generator emits the fixed WHO 5-band scale for all UV rows
(`generate_master_atlas_excel.py`), never Equal Interval. Effective UV coverage starts
2001 (CERES availability); a run-time warning is logged when the requested start is earlier.

### A.2 Heat Index: spring/autumn/seasonal-range
`compute_thermal_stress_fields` additionally returns `HI_Spring_Mean` (MAM),
`HI_Autumn_Mean` (SON) via Rothfusz, and `HI_Seasonal_Range` (max−min of the four
seasonal means). Registered in `FIELD_DEFS`, `SHP_FIELD_MAP`
(`HI_SprMean`/`HI_AutMean`/`HI_SeaRng`), offline backfill, QA ranges, raster mirrors,
`generate_excel_dictionary.py` (module count 5→8) and `migrate_legacy_database.py`.

### A.3 Wind Chill opt-in cold-regions suite (default OFF)
`compute_winter_chill_fields` returns spring/summer/autumn means, annual and seasonal
ranges, and the 12 monthly means. `FIELD_DEFS` + `SHP_FIELD_MAP`
(`WC_SprMean`/`WC_SumMean`/`WC_AutMean`/`WC_AnRng`/`WC_SeaRng`,
`WC_JanMean`…`WC_DecMean`) document all 19; the `Extended_WC_Seasons` parameter
(`Wind Chill Full Suite for Cold Regions…`, default False) gates publication via
`wanted_fields_by_module` stripping, so default runs stay byte-identical (Winter+Annual).
Raster mirror (`Toolboxes/raster_atlas_generator.py`) applies the same gate; dictionary count 2→19.

### A.4 Optional Daily sheet
`Save_Daily_Series` (`GPBoolean`, default False): with Temporal=Daily, `_build_daily_table`
emits long-format daily rows into a third `Daily` workbook sheet per element; the Month
sheet is always kept. Beyond ~60000 rows a `<Element>_daily.csv` is written instead.

- `LST_minus_T_Spring` (SHP: `LST_mT_Spr`): Spring surface-to-air temperature difference (`LST_Spring_Mean - T_Spring_Mean`, °C).
- `LST_minus_T_Summer` (SHP: `LST_mT_Sum`): Summer surface-to-air temperature difference (`LST_Summer_Mean - T_Summer_Mean`, °C).
- `LST_minus_T_Autumn` (SHP: `LST_mT_Aut`): Autumn surface-to-air temperature difference (`LST_Autumn_Mean - T_Autumn_Mean`, °C).
- 12 Monthly Means `LST_January_Mean` ... `LST_December_Mean` (SHP: `LST_JanMn` ... `LST_DecMn`): Climatological mean skin temperature for each calendar month (°C). Lives in GDB and Excel `Month` sheet.
- 12 Monthly Differences `LST_minus_T_January` ... `LST_minus_T_December` (SHP: `LST_mT_Jan` ... `LST_mT_Dec`): Monthly surface skin minus air temperature differences (GDB and Excel `Month` sheet only).
*(Note: Redundant fields `LST_Month_Mean` and `LST_Seasonal_Range` have been retired in line with the streamlined schema)*.
Raster tool produces the 11 primary indicator rasters (`LST_Annual_Mean`, 4 seasons, Annual Range, 5 differences) into `19_Land_Surface_Temperature/`.
All fields have English names and aliases equal to field names (`field_alias = field name`).
