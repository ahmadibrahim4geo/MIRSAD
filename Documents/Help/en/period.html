Set inclusive start/end dates as YYYY-MM-DD. Point downloads are daily and can cover partial periods; a complete scientific month requires every calendar day. A few days are not promoted into a full-month/year total. Partial-period fields such as R_Period_Total and T_Period_Mean differ from annual climate summaries. Coverage_Fraction measures availability, not accuracy.

Select Annual and required seasons; monthly fields remain in workbooks. Definitions are DJF=12/1/2, MAM=3/4/5, JJA=6/7/8 and SON=9/10/11. In an individual-year workbook, the current kernel groups December, January and February from that same exported calendar year, rather than previous December plus next January/February. Monthly interannual range compares the same month across years; it is not a single-day range.

Choose Metric/Imperial or individual temperature, precipitation, pressure, wind and solar units. Read Measurement_Unit and the Daily sheet's Unit: daily records use normalized source units and can differ from chosen summary units. Temperature differences and absolute temperatures have different Celsius/Fahrenheit conversions. Daily solar energy per area per day is not a W/m² power value unless explicitly identified as such.
