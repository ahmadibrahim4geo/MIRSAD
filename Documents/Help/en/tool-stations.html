1. Select national-network or spatial/custom station selection, then region/country or study boundary.
  2. In the Toolbox an empty station list can mean the whole national network depending on selection mode. Desktop requires explicit working-point selection; visible rows are not selected rows.
  3. Local station and supplementary point layers are supported. Keep USAF and WBAN as text plus location; USAF alone is not unique.
  4. Choose standalone NOAA, standalone ERA5, Hybrid or a suitable gridded source. Hybrid completes NOAA for all sites, then ERA5 for gaps, then NASA, then optional monthly filling.
  5. Check station count, region, dates and coverage before a large run. Catalogue coordinates do not constitute field verification.
